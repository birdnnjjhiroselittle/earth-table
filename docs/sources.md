## Hunger / 飢餓

- FAO, IFAD, UNICEF, WFP, WHO. *The State of Food Security and Nutrition in the World 2025* (SOFI 2025): about 673 million people (8.2%) faced hunger in 2024 (range 638–720 million).
  - https://data.unicef.org/resources/sofi-2025/
  - https://www.downtoearth.org.in/health/around-82-of-worlds-population-affected-by-hunger-last-year-sofi-2025

## Food waste / 食品廃棄

- UNEP. *Food Waste Index Report 2024*: 1.05 billion tonnes of food wasted in 2022, about 19% of food available to consumers; 60% from households, 28% food service, 12% retail. Food loss and waste generate an estimated 8–10% of global greenhouse gas emissions.
  - https://news.un.org/en/node/1148036

## Obesity / 肥満

- WHO / NCD Risk Factor Collaboration (The Lancet, 2024): more than 1 billion people worldwide are living with obesity.
  - https://www.who.int/news/item/01-03-2024-one-in-eight-people-are-now-living-with-obesity
